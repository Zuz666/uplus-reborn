# Appliance terminology — HWD120-B1558U

Manual-backed Russian ↔ English machine vocabulary, with a separately labelled
comparison against the exact-model Haier U+ APK programme catalogue. This is a
terminology reference, not an operating procedure or a list of independently
verified appliance behaviours. Application notes distinguish uses of a word;
they do not reproduce the manuals' instructions or programme-setting tables.

EN-UM identifies **HWD120-B1558U** in its technical-data table, §11.1 p. 40
(PDF p. 41). The shorter **HWD120-B1558** on its cover does not make it a
different-model manual: EN-UM is an exact-model English user-manual source here.
Its chapter 8 is Care and cleaning, not Haier U+: the English Haier U+ terms
below still come from EN-SVC chapter 8. A citation identifies the manual and
section without requiring access to a private research workspace.

APK-EN is a separate application-wording source, not a fourth manual. The
inspected APK has SHA-256
`30fd548089468cddd9b5ccce04643f9529d70016e748141d04c70f593a0ced9e`.
Programme resource IDs were resolved through its resource table to English
strings and restricted to the exact target's two catalogues. A string used by
another model, or merely present elsewhere in the APK, is not a target programme.
This comparison does not establish Russian APK wording or a live-rendered UI.

- **EN primary** follows the user manual where it supplies a term. **EN alt**
  records a distinct source form, not a newly coined synonym. A dash means no
  separately established term in that column, not that the feature is absent.
- User-manual wording takes priority over service-manual wording. Different
  labels remain visible; matching table positions/usages are a terminology
  correspondence, not proof that programme encodings or settings are identical.
- Terms retain source spelling, including `Шелк` and `Легкая глажка`. Layout-only
  line breaks and word hyphenation are joined; a visually Cyrillic С in `Сушка`
  is written as Cyrillic, even where a PDF text layer encodes the same glyph as C.
- Two explicitly authorised spelling corrections are used as RU primary forms:
  **Интенсивная** and **Автоматический**. Their literal source forms and locations
  remain in [Source differences](#source-differences-and-editorial-corrections).
  No other corrected spelling is implied by those exceptions.
- A programme name and an option or process word can share the same spelling.
  **Application** contains original English usage notes by U+ Reborn contributors,
  based on the cited manuals: it is neither a verbatim appliance label nor an
  APK programme name. **APK EN** is the separate, source-backed comparison
  column; it does not override **EN primary**. Numeric values below describe
  displayed choices, not a per-programme capability map.

### Conclusions from the APK comparison

- The manual-derived EN primary terms remain primary; APK wording is evidence of
  application labels, not a reason to replace the user manual's terminology.
- `Delicate Care` is not a unique cross-drum programme name: the APK uses it for
  both upper Silk and lower Delicates. Preserve their drum/manual context instead
  of merging the two terms or treating the name alone as a programme identity.
- Color Wear / Colored laundry and Tub Clean / Drum Clean are source-specific
  name differences; Kids´Wear / Kids' Wear and Rinse & Spin / Rinse+Spin also
  differ typographically. The table preserves each actual spelling.
- The APK uses Hygiene and Outdoor Care, agreeing with EN-UM rather than the
  service manual's Sanitary and Outdoor alternatives.
- The manuals' simultaneous 12 kg Standard remains part of the glossary despite
  not being listed in the inspected APK catalogues. Catalogue absence does not
  remove a documented appliance term or prove the feature unavailable.

## Selected base terminology

These are the selected full semantic bases, before any later display-name
shortening. The source catalogue above remains verbatim evidence, including
alternatives; a listed alternative is not an additional selected term. Selection
does not establish a wire encoding, programme capability or independently
verified behaviour.
